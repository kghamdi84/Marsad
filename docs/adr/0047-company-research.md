# ADR 0047: Company research from Tauric (analyst consensus, profile, Tauric AI thesis, calendar)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-28

## Context

Khaled showed TipRanks' NVDA research pages (analyst forecasts, AI stock analysis, key statistics, business overview, corporate events, dividends, earnings) and asked for them in Marsad. Massive's free plan and the SEC give none of analyst consensus, price targets, a company description or forward estimates. Tauric (ADR 0041) does, on the Standard tier Khaled already has. This ADR covers the data layer; the Study company page sections are built against the same storage contract.

What was confirmed on 2026-11-28 (a Saturday) from the public `https://api.tauric.ai/openapi.json` and 13 Tauric MCP calls (the connector runs the same operations and schemas as the REST routes):

| Part               | REST route                                                 | Schema             | Credit                                             |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Analyst consensus  | `GET /v1/securities/{symbol}/analyst`                      | `AnalystConsensus` | 1 data credit per 200                              |
| Profile            | `GET /v1/securities/{symbol}/profile`                      | `SecurityProfile`  | 1 data credit per 200                              |
| Thesis (Tauric AI) | `GET /v1/securities/{symbol}/thesis` (optional `known_at`) | `ThesisResponse`   | 1 data credit per 200 (data pool, not the AI pool) |
| Calendar           | `GET /v1/securities/{symbol}/calendar`                     | `SecurityCalendar` | 1 data credit per 200                              |
| Account usage      | `GET /v1/account`                                          | `AccountView`      | none (`quota: exempt`)                             |

- `{symbol}` is a ticker (`AAPL`) or `MIC:SYMBOL`; auth is `Authorization: Bearer`, as for quotes. Every route is marked `x-tauric-operation: {quota: data, entitlement: data}`.
- Credits were measured, not assumed: `get_account` read 4 data credits used before and 10 after six successful reads (NVDA and AAPL, three parts each); the three `not_registered` answers for BBAI cost nothing. The rate card says "Data request: 1 credit per 1".
- Errors are `{"error": {type, code, message, param, retry}}`. A 404 carries `not_registered`, `not_supported`, `not_published`, `insufficient_history`, `not_found` or `delisted`; 503 is `temporarily_unavailable`; 429 is `rate_limit_exceeded` or `quota_exhausted`.
- Fields: the analyst part gives `recommendation` (five-point scale), `target_mean_price`, `target_high_price`, `target_low_price`, `target_median_price`, `number_of_analysts`, `ratings_total` (the sum of the five counts), `strong_buy`, `buy`, `hold`, `sell`, `strong_sell`, `trailing_eps`, `forward_eps`. The profile gives GICS classification with keys, market cap with its basis and price date, valuation multiples, margins, returns, growth (fractions), cash, debt, liquidity ratios, dividend rate, yield and payout, beta, share counts, employees, website, description, short interest and ownership. The thesis gives `content.narrative`, `drivers[]`, `risks[]` (text, claim_id, article_ids, digest refs), `catalysts[]` (date, text, timing_stated, timing_status), `open_questions[]`, `generated_at`, `evidence.cutoff`, `revision`, `authorship: tauric_ai`. Each part has `dataset_status.refreshed_at`, except the thesis (`generated_at`).
- The calendar gives the next earnings date with **one** consensus EPS and revenue estimate (`earnings.earnings_date`, `currency`, `eps_estimate`, `revenue_estimate`), and the latest stated dividend ex-date with its amount when known. Its schema states "reported actuals and surprises are unavailable". On the recording day NVDA's `earnings` was null with `field_states.earnings: temporarily_unavailable`; AAPL's read 2027-01-28, EPS 2.9106, revenue 156,429,989,490 USD.
- Observed refresh times: analyst and profile data at 00:31 (AAPL) and 01:46 UTC (NVDA); theses written at 08:20 and 08:34 UTC with an evidence cutoff seconds earlier.
- Not provided: per-analyst ratings and track records, rating history, earnings-call transcripts, revenue by segment or geography, reported EPS and surprise, more than one quarter of estimates. Tauric does not carry BBAI (and, per ADR 0041, MARA, HIMS, RGTI).
- Vendor terms (tauric.ai/terms, effective 2026-11-11, reviewed for ADR 0041): "You may use and display data and content from the Services in your work and applications", no resale, no sharing of keys, no SLA.

## Decision

1. **One table, `public.company_research`**, one row per ticker: `analyst`, `profile`, `thesis`, `calendar` (jsonb, the validated payload minus `instrument`, or null), `analyst_refreshed_at`, `profile_refreshed_at`, `thesis_generated_at`, `calendar_refreshed_at`, `fetched_at`, and `errors` (jsonb, per part: the vendor code such as `not_registered` or `temporarily_unavailable`, Marsad's `malformed: <reason>`, or a failure kind such as `rate-limited`). The stored thesis is `{narrative, drivers, risks, catalysts, open_questions, generated_at, cutoff, revision}`; drivers and risks keep `text`, `claim_id` and `article_ids`. The calendar's as-of time is the earlier of its earnings and dividends refresh times.
2. **Validated in core, per part** (`packages/core/src/company-research.ts`, `tauric-research-1`): numbers finite; counts whole and non-negative; the five rating counts add up to `ratings_total`; targets above zero and ordered low <= median <= high with the mean inside; amounts and share counts non-negative; float not above shares outstanding; the website an http(s) address; dates `YYYY-MM-DD`; times with a zone; catalyst dates present exactly when not `undated`; the evidence cutoff not after the writing time; authorship `tauric_ai`; text within size limits and free of control characters. A malformed part is rejected whole with its reason, never repaired. The web app runs the same validators on what it reads.
3. **What is kept on failure.** A settled answer (the not-available codes) clears the part, so nothing stale lingers for a ticker Tauric dropped. A passing failure (outage, rate limit, malformed payload) keeps the stored part and its refreshed time, and records the reason in `errors`. The page shows each part's own date, so an old part reads as old.
4. **Job `tauric-research`** (`jobs/src/tauric-research-job.ts`, `.github/workflows/tauric-research.yml`), daily at 09:35 UTC Tuesday to Saturday: after each session's close and Tauric's overnight refresh, before the next open. It reads holdings first, then saved watchlists. It reads the day's credit use from `GET /v1/account` (free), keeps 300 credits for the quotes and reconcile jobs, and spends at most 300 a run (75 tickers at 4 credits; tickers past the budget are listed as deferred). It warns in the run details when the day's use reaches 80% of the allowance. The profile is read first: a ticker Tauric does not carry stops there, at no credit. A rejected key, a missing entitlement or a spent quota stops the run as an error. Each run writes `job_runs` with per-ticker, per-part outcomes, credits before and spent, the budget, and lists of not covered, deferred, malformed and failed parts. It shares the `tauric-api` concurrency group and waits for the `TAURIC_API_KEY` secret like the quotes job. Supabase Cron starts it (`gh-tauric-research`, ADR 0008).
5. **Members read it** (friends, ADR 0040), with the same `private.is_member()` policy (MFA rule included) as `price_quotes`. Showing a friend Tauric's data inside Marsad is the display the terms allow; no one pays, no key is shared, nothing is resold or exported in bulk. If Marsad ever charges anyone, or the terms change, this becomes owner-only. No client writes; no grants to `anon`.
6. **Labels.** The thesis is Tauric AI's text: "Written by Tauric AI on <date>, evidence to <cutoff>. Not Marsad's view, not advice." The profile description is labelled as Tauric's. The consensus word is the vendor's word for the analysts' view, shown with its counts and date. All vendor text is plain text; it is never markup, never a prompt instruction, and never run.

7. **Analyst history, `public.analyst_history`** (migration `20270105000200`). Tauric serves only today's consensus, so a trigger on `company_research` copies each analyst write into one row per ticker per UTC day (rating counts, analyst count, mean, median, high and low targets, refreshed time); a second write the same day replaces that day's row, and clearing the part keeps the history. The recommendation trend on the Study page reads it and says when the history starts. Same member-read policy; nobody signed in writes.
8. **Not on Khaled's plan (checked 2026-11-28 with the Massive MCP, `NOT_ENTITLED`).** Per-analyst ratings, firms, track records and estimate ranges (Benzinga `/benzinga/v1/ratings`, `/analysts`, `/earnings`, `/consensus-ratings`) are a paid partner add-on. Marsad shows "Not available from Marsad's sources" for them; adding the add-on is a cost decision for Khaled.

## Consequences

- A run spends at most 4 credits per covered ticker (48 for a dozen); the cap keeps the worst case at 300 of 3,000, and the quotes job keeps its reserve.
- No `source_status` row: no rule or alert reads this table, so its silence pauses nothing. A failed or missing run shows on the Status page through `job_runs`, and every part carries its own date.
- The next-quarter EPS and revenue estimates are a single consensus figure; Marsad still has no surprise or estimate history (checklist 2.6 stays as it was for those).
- The fixtures (`fixtures/tauric/research-{NVDA,AAPL,BBAI}-2026-11-28.json`) were recorded through the MCP connector; re-record from the REST API once the key is set, as ADR 0041 says for quotes.
- Vendor risk is the same as ADR 0041: no SLA, change or stop at any time. Losing Tauric removes these sections and nothing else.
